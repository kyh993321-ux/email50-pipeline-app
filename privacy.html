Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

email50-pipeline is a private tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google accounts.

Data accessed

  • Gmail (scope gmail.modify): reads messages in the owner's mailbox, adds labels to processed messages, and sends a briefing email to the owner.
  • Google Sheets (scope spreadsheets): reads and writes the owner's own spreadsheet that stores the summaries.

Use and sharing

Data is used only to produce summaries for the owner. It is not sold, not shared with other people, and not used for advertising. Message text may be sent to a language-model API chosen by the owner solely to generate the summaries.

Storage

Sign-in tokens and results are kept on the owner's own computer and Google Cloud virtual machine, and in the owner's own spreadsheet. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
